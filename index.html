Authentic Srikalahasti (Sreekalahasthi) Hand-Painted Kalamkari Sarees

Welcome to Kailash Kalamkari Srikalahasthi Pen Kalamkari. Crafted in the holy temple town of Srikalahasti (Sreekalahasthi), Andhra Pradesh, our collection features 100% genuine pen Kalamkari sarees, pure silk sarees, dupattas, and fabrics. Every artwork is drawn freehand using natural organic dyes and traditional bamboo pens (kalam) directly by master artisans since 1984.
